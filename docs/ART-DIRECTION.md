# DIRECTIVE V — Art Direction

## Concept
**MILITARY INTELLIGENCE × CINEMATIC WARFARE**

The website should feel like entering a classified military system that gradually becomes compromised.

The aesthetic is not “futuristic UI for its own sake.” Every visual element should contribute to information, atmosphere or story.

---

## Visual feeling
- Dark
- Tactical
- Cinematic
- Controlled
- Dangerous
- Premium
- Photorealistic
- Near-future

Dark does not mean pure black everywhere. Preserve shadow detail and visual depth.

---

## Palette

### Void
`#08090A`
Primary background.

### Carbon
`#141618`
Elevated surfaces and secondary backgrounds.

### Ash
`#A3A6A8`
Metadata, supporting copy, coordinates, labels.

### Bone
`#E8E5DF`
Primary text.

### Signal Red
`#C92A2A`
Semantic accent.

Red means:
- THREAT
- ACTIVE
- TARGET
- WARNING
- DOMINION
- CLASSIFIED

If red appears everywhere, the system has failed.

---

## Typography

### Display
Strong condensed grotesk territory.
Use for massive cinematic statements:
- DIRECTIVE V
- FIVE GO IN.
- THE BLACKOUT.
- THEY NEVER LEFT.
- MEET RAVEN.
- PROJECT ECLIPSE.

### Body
Neutral, highly readable grotesk/sans.

### System
Monospace for:
- timestamps
- coordinates
- classifications
- operator numbers
- system states
- countdowns
- metadata

Example:
`OPERATOR // 03`
`STATUS // ACTIVE`
`02:17:00 UTC`

---

## Scale
Use strong contrast between:
- extremely large display typography
- small precise system metadata

Do not make every text element medium-sized.

---

## Grid
Desktop:
- 12-column grid
- generous margins
- generous negative space
- cinematic media may bleed outside grid
- metadata remains aligned

The composition should feel like order imposed over chaos.

---

## UI language
Avoid soft SaaS patterns.

Prefer:
- square/sharp geometry
- 0–2px radii
- thin rules
- line extensions
- brackets
- `+`
- `×`
- `//`
- `[ ]`
- coordinates
- measurement ticks
- restrained crosshair motifs

Example CTA:
`ENTER BRIEFING →`

not a generic rounded pill.

---

## Image treatment
Images should feel like frames from a premium military thriller.

Use:
- partial faces
- silhouettes
- negative space
- rain
- fog
- snow
- dust
- smoke
- wet surfaces
- environmental depth
- restrained film grain

Not every operator image needs to show the entire body.

---

## Lighting language

### TF-5
Cold white / steel-blue ambient lighting.
Represents discipline and control.

### Dominion
Deep red / amber / harsh industrial lighting.
Represents threat and instability.

### Eclipse
Near-black with severe white light and restrained red/system accents.
Should feel like escalation without becoming fantasy sci-fi.

---

## Texture
Subtle only:
- film grain
- dust
- lens moisture
- scratches
- scan lines
- compression artifacts
- document/paper texture where appropriate
- fingerprints

Users should often feel texture before consciously noticing it.

---

## Navigation
Avoid generic:
Home / About / Characters / Gameplay / Contact

Preferred information language:
- `01 INTEL`
- `02 CONFLICT`
- `03 OPERATORS`
- `04 OPERATIONS`
- `05 PROTOCOL`

Primary action:
`ENTER BRIEFING`

Navigation begins minimal/transparent and may resolve into a restrained dark tactical bar on scroll.

---

## Logo
Typography-led identity.

Preferred territory:
`DIRECTIVE // V`

The V is the primary symbol.

Avoid:
- skulls
- guns
- eagles
- generic military crests

---

## Motion

### Cinematic
Slow:
- subtle zooms
- parallax
- fog
- rain
- light sweeps
- image masks
- camera pushes

Typical duration: 3–8 seconds.

### Interface
Fast:
- labels
- lines
- cursor changes
- counters
- tactical markers

Typical duration: 100–250ms.

### Story
Major transitions can temporarily control the whole viewport.

Example Blackout:
`02:16:57`
`02:16:58`
`02:16:59`
`02:17:00`
black
then system failure.

### Glitch
Rare.
Use when Dominion interrupts the system and heavily during Operator 06.

The first half of the site should feel controlled enough that later corruption matters.

---

## Cursor concept
Desktop optional:
- Normal: `+`
- Interactive: `[ + ]`
- Operator: `[ ID ]`
- Intel: `[ OPEN ]`

Do not compromise usability.

---

## Sound direction
Audio is optional and opt-in.

Potential layers:
- low atmospheric rumble
- radio static
- rain
- distant aircraft
- equipment clicks
- system feedback
- encrypted transmissions
- short character dialogue

The page must remain excellent when muted.

---

## Emotional progression
1. CONTROL
2. UNEASE
3. THREAT
4. POWER
5. WAR
6. URGENCY
7. CONFUSION
8. SILENCE

This emotional arc should influence pacing, color, sound and motion.

---

## Primary rule
> If an element doesn't contribute to information, atmosphere or story, remove it.
