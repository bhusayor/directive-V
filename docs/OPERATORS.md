# DIRECTIVE V — RAVEN Operator Bible

The supplied character images in `/public/assets/characters` are the visual reference for these operators. Preserve their established identities.

# 01 — WARDEN

## Elias “WARDEN” Kane
- Role: Squad Leader / Tactical Commander
- Age: 38
- Nationality: British-Nigerian
- Height: 6'1" / 185 cm
- Build: athletic, powerful, not excessively bulky

### Personality
Calm, controlled and disciplined. Warden does not need to shout to command authority. The squad trusts his decisions.

His emotional weakness is responsibility: he carries the weight of every operator he loses.

### Combat
Primary: modular assault rifle  
Secondary: heavy sidearm  
Equipment: tactical command interface, smoke grenades, combat knife

### Signature ability
**COMMAND LINK**

Shares enemy markings and tactical information across the squad for improved coordination.

### Stats
- Tactical IQ: 96
- Accuracy: 89
- Strength: 82
- Stamina: 84
- Mobility: 73
- Stealth: 64

### Visual identity
- dark brown skin
- close-cropped black hair
- short beard with slight grey
- healed scar through right eyebrow
- medium-heavy armor
- distinctive vertical crimson command stripe
- experienced, controlled presence

Suggested weapon designation: **ARX-41**

# 03 — ATLAS

## Viktor “ATLAS” Volkov
- Role: Heavy Weapons / Defense
- Age: 41
- Nationality: Ukrainian
- Height: 6'5" / 196 cm
- Build: extremely large / muscular

### Personality
Physically intimidating but one of the warmest members of Raven. Often jokes during combat. When Atlas stops joking, the situation is serious.

### Combat
Primary: belt-fed light machine gun  
Secondary: large-caliber handgun  
Equipment: reinforced protection, breaching equipment

### Signature ability
**HOLD THE LINE**

Deploys/uses reinforced protection and greatly increases survivability under incoming fire for a limited period.

### Stats
- Strength: 99
- Stamina: 93
- Tactical IQ: 74
- Accuracy: 72
- Mobility: 52
- Stealth: 31

### Visual identity
- shaved head
- heavy beard
- broken nose healed slightly crooked
- old facial scars
- kind eyes
- heaviest armor in the team
- industrial support/exoskeleton elements, not superhero armor
- scratched `5/5` marking on armor

Suggested weapon designation: **MG-76 “HAMMER”**

# Team relationships

## Warden ↔ Ghost
Deep professional trust. Ghost's information is enough for Warden to act.

## Atlas ↔ Cipher
Brother-like dynamic. Cipher teases Atlas about technology; Atlas treats every drone as a “little helicopter.”

## Pulse ↔ Atlas
Pulse is one of the few people who can order Atlas around when he is injured.

## Warden ↔ Pulse
Occasional philosophical conflict:
- Warden prioritizes completing the mission.
- Pulse prioritizes bringing everyone home.
