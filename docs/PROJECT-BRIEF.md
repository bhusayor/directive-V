# DIRECTIVE V — Project Brief

## What we are building
DIRECTIVE V is a fictional near-future tactical war-shooter concept.

The deliverable is a **cinematic promotional landing page only**. No playable game, matchmaking, backend game systems or real gameplay need to be built.

The page should be convincing enough that a visitor could believe they are viewing the reveal site for an upcoming AAA tactical shooter.

## Working title
**DIRECTIVE V**

## Primary tagline
**FIVE GO IN. FIVE COME OUT.**

## Setting
Year: **2053**

The world has not ended; it has fractured. Nations and ordinary civilian life still exist, but modern infrastructure and warfare have become deeply dependent on satellites, autonomous systems, connected defense networks, communications and energy infrastructure.

The central idea:
> You do not need to invade a country if you can switch it off.

Technology should feel like a believable extension of the present: recognizable weapons and equipment enhanced by better drones, optics, electronic warfare, battlefield AI and communications. Avoid distant-future fantasy.

## Product fantasy
The fictional game supports:
- Story-driven campaign
- Five-player cooperative missions
- Objective-based 5v5 competitive multiplayer

The landing page should reveal those ideas through atmosphere and narrative rather than long feature descriptions.

## Core squad
Directive Five created autonomous five-person tactical teams capable of functioning when command structures and connected technology fail.

The featured team is:
- Unit: TF-5
- Callsign: RAVEN

Operators:
1. WARDEN
2. GHOST
3. ATLAS
4. CIPHER
5. PULSE

## Primary antagonist
**Dominion**

A decentralized military organization built from former intelligence personnel, soldiers, engineers, mercenaries and other operatives. It is not tied to one real-world nation.

Its core idea:
> Control the system. Control the world.

## Antagonist figure
**ARCHITECT**
- Identity unknown
- Nationality unknown
- Location unknown
- Status active

Associated line:
> Order follows silence.

## Central threat
**PROJECT ECLIPSE**

Dominion is preparing a larger version of the capability demonstrated during the Blackout.

The campaign begins with:
**ECLIPSE ACTIVATION: 72 HOURS**

The squad must locate Dominion, discover what Eclipse is, prevent activation and survive.

## Mystery
A captured Dominion terminal contains complete TF-5 records.

Five expected operators appear as active.

Then:
**OPERATOR 06 — [REDACTED]**

This creates the landing page's final narrative hook.

## Experience objective
The visitor should discover the world in stages:
1. Enter a secure briefing.
2. Meet Raven visually.
3. Learn about the Blackout.
4. Discover Dominion.
5. Understand Directive Five.
6. Meet the operators individually.
7. Discover Project Eclipse.
8. See mission environments.
9. Discover 5v5.
10. Encounter Operator 06.
11. Lose the connection.

The page should feel closer to an interactive mission briefing than a traditional marketing template.
