# DIRECTIVE V — Story & World

## 2053
Modern civilization is dependent on deeply interconnected infrastructure: power, communications, transportation, satellites, financial systems, drones and defense networks.

War evolved with it. Small tactical teams, electronic warfare, cyber operations, autonomous systems and precision strikes can now achieve effects once requiring conventional armies.

The world remains recognizable. DIRECTIVE V is near-future military realism, not space opera.

---

## THE BLACKOUT

### Date
**17 November 2049**

### Time
**02:17 UTC**

Military satellites, communication networks and power infrastructure across multiple regions failed simultaneously.

For exactly:
**11 minutes, 42 seconds**

military systems began behaving as if they were receiving legitimate commands that nobody had issued.

Effects included:
- autonomous defense activation
- drone launches
- false military orders
- targeting failures
- communications collapse
- infrastructure disruption
- friendly units incorrectly identified as hostile

When control was restored, major damage had already occurred.

Nobody publicly claimed responsibility.

The event became known as:
**THE BLACKOUT**

`02:17` becomes a recurring visual/narrative motif throughout the website.

## DOMINION

Dominion is not a nation.

It is a decentralized military organization containing former intelligence officers, engineers, soldiers, mercenaries and specialists from multiple backgrounds.

It believes civilization's dependence on centralized digital infrastructure is both a weakness and an opportunity.

Core principle:
> Control the system. Control the world.

Dominion's visual identity should feel repurposed and denationalized:
- manufacturer markings removed
- serial numbers scratched away
- mixed equipment
- charcoal / ash / dirty white
- controlled crimson identifiers

Dominion should not be represented as a real nationality or real political faction.

### Dominion phrase
> Order follows silence.

## DIRECTIVE FIVE

After the Blackout, military planners concluded that large connected command structures had become dangerously vulnerable.

Directive Five was established to create autonomous five-person units capable of continuing operations when:
- communications fail
- satellites disappear
- command structures collapse
- digital systems are compromised

Small enough to disappear.
Skilled enough to change a war.

### Featured unit
**TF-5 / RAVEN**

Unofficial rule:
> Five go in. Five come out.

## PROJECT ECLIPSE

Four years after the Blackout, Dominion appears to return.

Signals include:
- a satellite disappearing from normal operation
- weapons facilities losing contact
- missing military convoys
- abandoned communication sites transmitting again
- repeated transmissions containing `02:17`

Cipher uncovers the central truth:

**The Blackout was not the final attack. It was a test.**

Dominion has spent four years developing Project Eclipse.

Initial intelligence:
**ECLIPSE ACTIVATION: 72 HOURS**

Raven's directive:
- Find Dominion.
- Identify Eclipse.
- Stop activation.
- Come home.

### Grounded interpretation
Eclipse should not become a giant fantasy space superweapon.

It is better treated as a distributed command/infrastructure compromise system capable of simultaneously affecting satellites, autonomous defenses, drones, communications, energy systems and military networks.

Narrative implication:
> The Blackout wasn't the weapon. It was the prototype.
